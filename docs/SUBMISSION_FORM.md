**Title:** Certified automatic winding constraints (99.6% precision, 1 miss in 636 two-wrap pairs) and TRACE, an ink detector trained on the eligible-protocol scans (+0.10 to +0.12 AUC held out)

**Team:** Jashanjot Singh Gill (github.com/Jashann), Nhat Nam Tran (github.com/nnm2602)

**Repository:** https://github.com/Jashann/vesuvius-scrolling (code MIT; weights, constraint files and fit grids CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, on the GitHub releases v1.0 and v1.1)

**Short description:**

Two tools, both aimed at the open problems for the eligible scrolls. Everything below is reproducible from the repository: `exp/e9_report.py` regenerates the certificate tables from the committed rows in seconds, `eval_trace.py` reproduces the ink table from public data in one command (the table quoted here is that command's output on a fresh clone), `exp/e31b_bootstrap.py` re-scores the published fit grids, and `pytest tests` checks that the quoted numbers follow from the result files.

1. PCU, certified winding constraints from Lasagna. We treat Lasagna's `cos` channel as the real part of a wrapped winding phase (a Riesz transform supplies the quadrature) and count wraps between two points three ways from that one prediction: local phase cycles, the wrap-density integral, and a whole-slice winding field unwrapped with min-cost-flow branch cuts after removing the umbilicus vortex analytically. A step is certified only when all three agree; a fourth vote from the recto-surface model gives a gold tier. On PHerc. Paris 4, against all 1,502 human adjacent-wrap pairs (63 slices): 99.62% precision at 70% coverage (4 errors in 1,052; 95% lower bound 99.0%; the best single measurement alone is 95.6%), 100% below 173 um wrap spacing, 99.7% on 20 held-out half-splits. The negative set that matters for a fit: on the 1,320 truly two-wrap pairs of the same ladders the certificate accepts 636 and calls 1 of them a one-wrap pair (98.6% precision; 94.9% on three-wrap pairs; a constant "1" would score 50% on the accepted set). Generated gold steps 942 / 942 against human ladders (lower bound 99.6%; the gold tier keeps 59% of certified steps). 377k gold pairs over 175 slices in VC3D point-collection format, loadable by the spiral fit as an extra relative-winding document. In the organisers' spiral fitter, fully automatic (no verified patches, no human windings): slips per wrap on Paris 4 from 20.3% to 9.3% on 32 held-out human ladders (ladder-level bootstrap CI 6.2 to 12.7%; paired difference 11 points, CI 7 to 16; a second random seed of both fits gives 22.1% to 8.8%; choosing the constraint weight on half the ladders and scoring the other half gives 10.2% vs 20.2%; human windings as input reach 3.0%), and on the eligible scroll PHerc0826 from 50.1% to 36.3% measured against PCU's own gold ladders on 8 held-out slices (agreement with PCU, not a human reference; our outer shell alone changes nothing, 49.5%). All fit grids and job scripts are published. Also a crest-graph synchronisation (2.7% ladder slips per wrap vs 5.1% for the best whole-slice unwrapping) and an annotation audit of 13 suspect human pairs. About 6 s per slice for generation on a laptop.

2. TRACE, an ink detector for the 9.362 um / 8.64 um protocols. ink_9um fine-tuned on the organisers' real coarse surface volumes of PHerc0139 and PHerc0814 with their 2.4 um ink predictions as soft targets, then extended with the public coarse-protocol fragment pairs (PHerc0009B, PHerc0343P at 8.64 um; PHerc0500P2 at 9.362 um). Held out on PHerc0841 (organisers' human labels, three segments, never trained on; checkpoints were selected on them, disclosed in the model card): AUC 0.746 / 0.736 / 0.705 for the released ink_9um (seed 43: 0.772 / 0.709 / 0.749; KLAVIS 0.799 / 0.807 / 0.762) vs 0.868 / 0.862 / 0.837 for TRACE-v3B; paired gain over ink_9um +0.10 to +0.12 with 1 cm block-bootstrap lower bounds of +0.05 to +0.06. Against sparse human labels on PHerc0139 w043 the fine-tunes score below the base model and KLAVIS (0.745 to 0.789 vs 0.825 and 0.851: they inherit the teacher's errors), and at the organisers' PHerc1447 ink site every public detector responds only weakly, the released model most (81st to 97th percentile of the crop; harness and full grid included). Use TRACE on 9.36 um segments; at 8.64 um there is no evidence it beats the base model.

Around them: an umbilicus estimator and band method for the eligible scrolls without a published centre line, a fit-plus-survey job for Kaggle (T4 x2) that fitted first bands of PHerc0191, 0257, 0358, 0813 and 0800, and a hotspot vetting protocol (depth profile, neighbouring-sheet control, lattice test) that showed eight of nine text-range hotspots on eligible scrolls to be regional texture. Negative results are documented with numbers (de-Paganin, flat fit-free patches, score-guided surface warping, synthetic ink insertion, dual-energy lead detection). No letters were read.

**Open problems addressed:** winding constraints; ink detection at the eligible protocol; label quality (annotation audit); false-positive control for ink surveys.
